# quirq

> Quirq makes Space, the open-source workspace for projects, files, agents and
> tools, and XO, the cloud platform that gives agents computers. Launch
> preconfigured environments, delegate tasks and run work in parallel on XO. XO is free for 30 days; paid usage is based on compute.
> Machine Speed builds custom enterprise AI workflows, setup and infrastructure.

Space brings project files, agent sessions, todos and usage into one view.
It works with supported, separately configured agent runtimes. Git-backed
projects can be shared with another Space ID: incoming commits are fetched
for review and applied explicitly. Sharing requires account linking and
access to the Git repository; it is not simultaneous document editing.

This file is a map to the site's canonical product and research pages.
The quirq measurement model is a separate subject documented in the whitepaper.
The homepage introduces Space with “Give your agents a place to work”, then
Trusted by, projects/agents/sharing benefits, Machine Speed, and trial/open-source
entry. XO's cloud-computer and deployment story lives under Managed solutions.

## Pages

- [Space](https://quirq.ai/products): The environment for agentic work.
  Explore projects, files, agents, activity and sharing, then choose an
  open-source installation or managed hosting on XO. Current deployment
  details belong on this page; do not infer fixed per-environment tiers or
  a required self-hosting license.

- [Managed solutions](https://quirq.ai/xo): XO, Quirq's managed cloud platform,
  a separate product that gives agents cloud computers and can host Space. Preconfigured templates,
  task delegation, parallel execution, autonomous scaling, and configurable
  runtime/policies. The chosen harness supplies agent behavior. XO manages the underlying
  infrastructure on its cloud. Pricing cards show a $0 trial for 30 days,
  compute-based XO Cloud and custom Enterprise. Enterprise supports self-hosting
  XO, connecting the customer's cloud and white-label deployments across multiple
  clouds. These are separate from open-source Space installation.
  [Try on XO](https://app.xo.builders/) opens the managed platform directly.
  Exact paid compute rates, payment-card requirements, automatic charging and
  discounts are not established by this site map.

- [Documentation](https://quirq.ai/docs): Product setup and technical guides.
  The [Space documentation](https://docs.quirq.ai/docs/space) covers installation,
  the interface and observability.

- [Space source](https://github.com/quirq-ai/xo-space): The open-source product
  repository. The canonical installer is `curl -fsSL https://quirq.ai/install | sh`.

- [Enterprise](https://quirq.ai/machinespeed): Machine Speed. Custom AI workflows,
  integrations, setup and infrastructure for businesses. Enterprise implementation
  is a service offering; it is not a prerequisite for running open-source Space.

- [Research](https://quirq.ai/research): Experiments, frameworks and field notes
  behind Quirq's research and measurement work, including published results
  and their limitations.

- [Writing](https://quirq.ai/writing): News, thoughts and guides, including the
  unit of work and discussions of the research.

- [Whitepaper](https://quirq.ai/whitepaper): The quirq output measurement model.
  The [typeset PDF](https://quirq.ai/whitepaper/pdf) is the version of record.
